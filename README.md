## Overview

*Into the Spider-Verse* is a **2018 animated superhero film** about Miles Morales, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and meets other Spider-People from different dimensions.

## Why I Love It

1. The animation style looks like a living comic book.
2. The soundtrack fits every scene.
3. The story is about **believing in yourself**.

## Main Characters

- Miles Morales
- Peter B. Parker
- Gwen Stacy
- Spider-Ham
